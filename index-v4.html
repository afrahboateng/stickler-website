The platform

From intake to close — one workflow

Every touchpoint of an off-market transaction unified into a single intelligent system, optimized for speed, trust, and capital deployment.

i.
Seller submits a property
Motivated sellers enter through Stickler's intake platform. Automated communication workflows and document verification prepare the deal at intake speed — no manual back-and-forth.
ii.
AI scores the deal instantly
The AI Property Intelligence Engine computes ARV, repair cost estimates, equity position, and a deal quality score — replacing weeks of due diligence with seconds of automated analysis.
iii.
Matched to verified buyers
The matching algorithm surfaces the opportunity to qualified, verified cash investors whose acquisition criteria align precisely. No cold outreach. No wasted capital or time.
iv.
Close in a digital deal room
Offers, negotiations, contracts, and closing coordination happen inside one unified experience. Every counterparty knows exactly where the deal stands, in real time.

Investment thesis

The largest untapped market in real estate

Off-market residential transactions represent the largest, most fragmented, and least digitized segment of the real estate market. No dominant platform player exists today.

Annual off-market transaction volume in the US

Data compounds over time
Every transaction through Stickler makes the platform smarter and more valuable. By Phase IV, the core asset is the proprietary dataset and network — neither of which can be replicated by a new entrant regardless of capital invested.
Revenue stacks at every phase
Transaction fees plus origination economics plus title plus insurance plus data licensing. Each service layer added increases revenue per transaction while simultaneously reducing friction — the rarest combination in marketplace design.
Network effects create the moat
More buyers improve matching accuracy. More transactions build pricing intelligence. More satisfied sellers become referral sources. The platform doesn't just grow — it improves as it grows, compounding into a structural advantage no late entrant can bridge.
Category definition, not competition
At full scale, Stickler is not competing with other marketplaces. It is the layer that all participants — agents, investors, lenders, servicers, and institutions — need to operate effectively.
