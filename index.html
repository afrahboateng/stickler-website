Getting started

How it's like working with stickler properties

1
Request Access
Submit your request to join our investor marketplace. A Stickler Properties Disposition Associate will contact you to discuss your investment goals, preferred markets, and buying criteria. This approval process helps us maintain an exclusive network of qualified investors while connecting you with opportunities that fit your strategy.
2
Explore Investment Opportunities
Once your access is approved, you'll be able to browse available investment properties within our marketplace. You can search for deals that align with your objectives and receive notifications when new opportunities become available.
3
Close Your Next Deal & Get funding
When you're ready to move forward, your Stickler Properties Disposition Associate will guide you through each stage of the transaction. From submitting an offer on your next investment property or applying for Funding.
Buy
1
Submit Your Property Information
Complete our property information form with as much detail as possible. The more information you provide, the better our AI-powered underwriting system can evaluate your home and prepare an accurate cash offer.
2
Schedule Your Home Appointment
Your selling goals matter to us. A Stickler Properties Acquisition Associate from your local market will visit your property, conduct a walkthrough, and explain our process. We'll answer your questions, present a no-obligation as-is cash offer, handle every aspect of the buying process, and can close in as little as 14 days.
3
Concierge service
Once you accept our offer, our transaction coordinators will guide you through the purchase agreement and closing process. They will ensure everything goes smoothly and work with you through the title and escrow process to make sure you get your wire or cashiers check on closing date.
