About us

The team behind Stickler

Operators, builders, and relationship makers — each bringing firsthand experience from the field of off-market real estate.

Isaac W. Stickler
Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Isaac W. Stickler is the driving force behind Stickler Properties, setting the firms vision, leading strategy, and overseeing execution across all business units. His background includes sales and marketing leadership, with a proven ability to build and manage high-performance teams in competitive environments.

With over 100+ distressed real estate projects completed, Mr.. Stickler brings operational expertise to every aspect of the business.

Mr. Stickler understands the mechanics of deal flow, the nuances of market timing, and the discipline needed to grow a real estate firm with consistency and precision.

As CEO, Mr. Stickler is responsible for expanding into new markets, cultivating investor relationships, and ensuring the Firms culture of excellence is reflected in every hire, deal, and partnership. His leadership philosophy focuses on empowering talented people, leveraging relationships, and maintaining high execution standards that turn vision into results.

Nathalie Truong
VP of Partnerships

Nathalie Truong serves as VP of Partnerships and is a core full-time member of the Stickler Properties executive team. She is the architect of the company's external relationship strategy — forging alliances with third-party wholesale real estate firms, traditional brokerages, and industry power players that expand the company's market reach and deal pipeline.

Nathalie's work sits at the intersection of business development and relationship management. She identifies high-value partnership opportunities, structures collaboration frameworks, and ensures that every strategic alliance is mutually beneficial and built for long-term durability. Her ability to navigate both the wholesale and traditional brokerage worlds gives Stickler Properties a unique competitive advantage in sourcing and distributing inventory.

As the company scales into multiple metro markets, Nathalie's partnerships function becomes increasingly critical — serving as a force multiplier that accelerates deal flow and strengthens Stickler Properties' reputation as a preferred institutional buyer and seller in each market it enters.

Ralph Beberniss
VP of Operations & Sales

Ralph Beberniss is an accomplished real estate entrepreneur and sales leader with a proven track record of execution and growth. He has completed more than 200 wholesale real estate transactions, bringing deep expertise across deal sourcing, acquisition, negotiation, and disposition. His hands-on experience in the field gives him a practical, results-driven perspective on every stage of the transaction process.

In addition to his real estate background, Ralph built and led a high-performing sales organization of more than 150 associates, where he developed scalable systems, training programs, and performance-driven processes that supported consistent growth. His ability to combine operational discipline with sales leadership has helped him create lasting value across multiple real estate markets.
