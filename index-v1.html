How it works

From lead to close — in one place

Every touchpoint of an off-market transaction, unified into a single intelligent workflow.

01
Seller submits a property
Motivated sellers enter their property through Stickler's intake platform. Automated communication workflows and document verification get the deal ready at intake speed.
02
AI scores the deal instantly
The AI Property Intelligence Engine computes ARV, repair cost estimates, equity position, and a deal quality score — replacing weeks of manual due diligence with seconds of automated analysis.
03
Matched to verified buyers
The buyer matching algorithm surfaces the opportunity to qualified, verified cash investors whose preferences align. No cold outreach. No wasted time.
04
Close in a digital deal room
Offers, negotiations, contracts, and closing coordination happen inside a single unified experience. Every stakeholder knows exactly where the deal stands, in real time.

From marketplace to operating system

A four-phase, seven-year build sequence. Each phase creates the preconditions for the next — no wasted steps.

1
0 – 12 months
Marketplace Foundation

Build the irreducible core: a connected marketplace where motivated sellers and qualified cash buyers can find each other, transact with confidence, and close faster than any traditional channel.

  • Seller acquisition platform
  • AI property intelligence engine
  • Verified buyer marketplace
  • Transaction management
2
12 – 24 months
Network Effects

Scale liquidity. More participants create better matches — and better matches attract more participants. The flywheel moment that makes the platform defensible.

  • Intelligent matching engine
  • Buyer operating system

24 – 48 months

Integrate lending, title, settlement, and insurance directly into the transaction workflow. Transform from a marketplace into a platform — and from a platform into infrastructure.

Real Estate OS

The investment thesis

A $1T+ market with no dominant player

Off-market residential transactions represent the largest, most fragmented, and least digitized segment of real estate. Stickler is the blueprint for becoming the dominant platform — methodically, defensibly, at scale.

Data compounds over time
Every transaction that flows through Stickler makes the platform smarter, faster, and more valuable to the next participant. By Phase 4, the core asset isn't the technology — it's the proprietary dataset and the network, neither of which can be replicated by a new entrant regardless of capital invested.
Revenue stacks at every phase
Transaction fees are a good business. Transaction fees plus origination economics plus title plus insurance plus data licensing is a great business. Each service layer added to the platform increases revenue per transaction while simultaneously reducing friction.
Network effects create the moat
More buyers improve matching accuracy. More transactions build pricing intelligence. More satisfied sellers become referral sources. The platform doesn't just grow — it improves as it grows, compounding into a structural advantage no late entrant can bridge.
